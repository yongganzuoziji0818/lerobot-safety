# Reference-to-claim matrix

| Claim role | Reference | Use in manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Taxonomy of risk uncertainty | Paté-Cornell (1996) | Establish that alternative treatments of uncertainty change the interpretation of risk output |
| Reliability under emerging complexity | Zio (2009) | Connect learning-enabled robots to broader reliability-engineering challenges |
| Decision-oriented uncertainty | Aven and Zio (2011) | Support matching uncertainty representation to the decision |
| Sampling uncertainty propagation | Helton et al. (2006) | Motivate explicit propagation and sensitivity reporting; not used as proof of bootstrap validity |
| ML assurance evidence chain | Paterson et al. (2025), AMLAS | Position contract validation as one evidence component, not a complete system safety case |
| Partial identification | Imbens and Manski (2004) | Distinguish identification regions from confidence intervals |
